What is Umrah?

Umrah is a voluntary act of worship performed in Makkah that includes entering the state of Ihram, performing Tawaf around the Kaaba, performing Sa'i between Safa and Marwah, and completing the pilgrimage by trimming or shaving the hair. Unlike Hajj, Umrah can generally be performed throughout the year (subject to local regulations and restrictions).

Step 1 — Prepare Before You Leave

A peaceful Umrah begins with good preparation.

Spiritual Preparation

  • Renew your intention solely for Allah.
  • Seek forgiveness before leaving.
  • Make peace with family and friends.
  • Learn the basic rulings of Umrah.
  • Memorize important duas and Talbiyah.

Travel Documents

  • Passport
  • Visa
  • Flight tickets
  • Hotel booking
  • Identification copies
  • Emergency contacts
  • Travel insurance (if applicable)

Essential Items

Ihram garments Comfortable sandals Small prayer mat Water bottle Unscented toiletries Medication Power bank Light backpack

Health Tips

  • Stay hydrated.
  • Wear comfortable footwear.
  • Carry prescribed medicines.
  • Avoid unnecessary physical exhaustion before Umrah.

Step 2 — Miqat & Entering Ihram

What is Miqat? Miqat is the designated boundary where pilgrims intending Umrah enter the sacred state of Ihram. Different travel routes have different Miqat locations.

Men's Ihram

  • Two white unstitched cloths.
  • One wrapped around the waist.
  • One covering the upper body.
  • Sandals that leave ankles exposed.

Women's Ihram

  • Modest Islamic clothing.
  • Face and hands generally remain uncovered.
  • Loose and comfortable clothing.

Before Crossing Miqat

  • Perform Ghusl (if possible).
  • Trim nails beforehand.
  • Wear Ihram garments.
  • Pray if appropriate.
  • Make the intention for Umrah.

Make Your Intention

"O Allah, I intend to perform Umrah. Make it easy for me and accept it from me."

Then begin reciting the Talbiyah.

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ
لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayk Allahumma Labbayk, Labbayka La Sharika Laka Labbayk, Innal Hamda Wan Ni'mata Laka Wal Mulk, La Sharika Lak.
Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner. Surely all praise, blessings, and dominion belong to You.

Step 3 — Arriving at Masjid al-Haram

Entering Masjid al-Haram is an emotional moment. Many pilgrims pause when seeing the Kaaba for the first time and make heartfelt supplications. Take a moment to appreciate the sacred surroundings before beginning Tawaf.

Step 4 — Tawaf Around the Kaaba

7 complete circuits around the Kaaba

Starting Point: Begin near the Black Stone (Hajar al-Aswad).

Direction: Walk counterclockwise around the Kaaba.

After Tawaf:

Pray 2 Rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim (if possible). Drink Zamzam water and make dua.

Step 6 — Hair Cutting

This marks the completion of Umrah.

Men

  • Shaving the head is highly rewarded.
  • Trimming is also permitted according to the relevant guidance.

Women

  • Trim a small portion of the hair as instructed according to the guidance followed.

After completing this step, you exit the state of Ihram.
